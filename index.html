Each point is a census tract in the selected area. The dark line is the median of the method within each tenth of the x-axis values. ρ is Spearman's rank correlation over the area's tracts. Click a point to select the tract on the maps.

This chart shows all three methods at once. Background colors are the M3 classes. Dashed diagonals are lines of equal ICE, since ICE is the high-income share minus the low-income share. Point color can show M2 entropy. Tracts in the lower left are middle-income, the upper right is mixed, and the other two corners are concentrated at one end.
